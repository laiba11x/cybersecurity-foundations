# CIA Triad

The **CIA Triad** is a core cybersecurity model made up of three principles:

* **Confidentiality**
* **Integrity**
* **Availability**

## Confidentiality

**Confidentiality** means that data can only be accessed by **authorised people**.

A breach happens when someone without permission accesses sensitive information.

Examples:

* Exposed passwords → confidentiality is **not achieved**.
* Company documents available only to employees who need them → confidentiality is **achieved**.

Common protections include **encryption** and **access controls**.

## Integrity

**Integrity** means that data cannot be **changed without authorisation**.

A breach happens when someone modifies data without permission.

Examples:

* Data changed with authorised approval → integrity is **achieved**.
* A student's attendance record changed after being locked → integrity is **not achieved**.
* An order price changed before checkout → integrity is **not achieved**.

## Availability

**Availability** means that data and services are **accessible to authorised users when needed**.

A breach happens when a service or data becomes unavailable.

Examples:

* A company's website goes offline during business hours → availability is **not achieved**.
* Systems are accessible to employees during working hours → availability is **achieved**.

Attacks such as **Denial-of-Service (DoS)** can affect availability by overwhelming a service with requests.

## Key Takeaway

The CIA Triad helps cybersecurity professionals protect:

* **Confidentiality** → prevent unauthorised access.
* **Integrity** → prevent unauthorised changes.
* **Availability** → keep systems and data accessible.
